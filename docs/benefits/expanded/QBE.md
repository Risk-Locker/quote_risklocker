## Table 1: QBE Private Car Insurance (Private Car Protector - 1 Year)
**Product Specification**: Private Car Protector
(System Type: Add-on System)
[Private Car - 1 Year Duration]

### Default Benefits (Included at RM 0 / Base Cover)
- **Own Damage**: Accidental collision, overturning, fire, explosion, lightning, burglary, theft, malicious act, Penang transit. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Third Party BI**: Unlimited coverage for third-party bodily injury and accidental death. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Third Party Property**: Third-party property damage coverage up to RM3,000,000. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Emergency Towing**: 24/7 towing assistance to nearest or preferred workshop or home. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Roadside Assistance**: 24/7 minor roadside repairs coverage up to RM500 per incident. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Betterment Waiver**: 0% betterment contribution on original parts for vehicles up to 10 years old. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Compassionate Allowance**: Total loss or theft payout of 5% sum insured up to RM5,000. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Key Replacement**: Lock/key replacement reimbursement up to RM500 for theft or break-in. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Legal Defense**: Court legal defense costs coverage up to RM2,000. **Cost**: 0 RM

### Add-on Benefits & Exact Base Cost
- **Windscreen**: Windscreen repair/replacement coverage up to RM1,000 (15% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **Special Perils**: Full flood, typhoon, landslide, natural storm coverage for RM50,000 car (0.25% rate). **Cost**: 125 RM
- **First Loss Flood**: Standalone first loss special perils natural disaster coverage up to RM10,000. **Cost**: 30 RM
- **SRCC**: Strike, riot and civil commotion damage coverage for RM50,000 car (0.30% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **LLP**: Legal liability to passengers for 4 passenger seats (RM5 per seat). **Cost**: 20 RM
- **LLOP**: Legal liability of passengers for third-party negligence. **Cost**: 7.50 RM
- **Accessories**: Non-standard accessories coverage up to RM1,000 (15% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **Out-of-Pocket**: Replacement car RM150/day up to 7 days, hotel RM250/day up to 5 days, whole car respray RM1,500. **Cost**: 90 RM
- **Flood Cleaning**: Water damage interior cleaning reimbursement up to RM1,500. **Cost**: 35 RM
- **All Drivers**: Waiver of RM400 compulsory excess for unnamed authorized drivers. **Cost**: 30 RM
- **CART**: Assessed repair time compensation of RM50/day for 7 days. **Cost**: 58.30 RM
- **Increased TPPD**: Upgrades third party property damage limit from RM3M to RM6M (+30% TP rate). **Cost**: 45 RM
- **Driver Passenger Protector**: Bundle personal accident coverage for driver and 4 passengers (Plan A). **Cost**: 70 RM
