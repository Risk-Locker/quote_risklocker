## Table 1: Lonpac Private Car Insurance (SecureDrive / Private Car Secure - 1 Year)
**Product Specification**: Private Car Secure
(System Type: Add-on System)
[Private Car - 1 Year Duration]

- **Third Party Property**: Third-party property damage coverage up to RM3,000,000. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Emergency Towing**: Accidental towing cost reimbursement up to RM200 to nearest approved repairer. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Transport Allowance**: PC Privilege 1 lump-sum transportation allowance of RM75 per payable own damage claim. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Natural Falling Objects**: PC Privilege 2 impact damage from falling objects due to convulsions of nature up to 25% of vehicle sum insured. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Document Replacement**: PC Privilege 3 smash-and-grab document replacement allowance up to RM150 for NRIC, passport, or driving license. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Windscreen Auto-Reinstatement**: PC Privilege 4 automatic reinstatement of original windscreen sum insured limit after repair. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Unnamed Driver Excess Waiver**: PC Privilege 5 waiver of RM400 compulsory excess for unnamed authorized drivers holding full license aged 21 and above. **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Betterment Scale**: Standard tariff betterment contribution scale (0% under 5 yrs up to 40% for 10+ yrs). **Cost**: 0 RM
- **Legal Defense**: Court legal defense costs coverage up to RM2,000. **Cost**: 0 RM

### Add-on Benefits & Exact Base Cost
- **Windscreen**: Endorsement 89 windscreen, windows, and sunroof repair or replacement coverage up to RM1,000 (15% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **Special Perils**: Endorsement 57 comprehensive flood, typhoon, storm, landslide coverage for RM50,000 vehicle (0.25% rate). **Cost**: 125 RM
- **Enhanced Special Perils**: Endorsement DT9 full natural disaster coverage including elevated towing allowance up to RM600. **Cost**: 140 RM
- **SRCC**: Endorsement 25 strike, riot, and civil commotion coverage for RM50,000 vehicle (0.30% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **LLP**: Endorsement 100 legal liability to passengers for 4 passenger seats (RM5 per seat). **Cost**: 20 RM
- **LLOP**: Endorsement 72 legal liability of passengers for third-party negligent acts. **Cost**: 7.50 RM
- **Accessories**: Endorsement 97 separate coverage for non-standard vehicle accessories up to RM1,000 (15% rate). **Cost**: 150 RM
- **Gas Kit & Tank**: Endorsement 97(a) separate insurance for installed NGV gas conversion kit and tank valued at RM2,000 (4.5% rate). **Cost**: 90 RM
- **CART**: Endorsement 112 compensation for assessed repair time of RM50/day for 7 days. **Cost**: 58.30 RM
- **NCD Relief**: Endorsement 111 compensation for forfeiture of current year NCD entitlement following an own-damage claim. **Cost**: 100 RM
- **Betterment Buyback**: Endorsement DT4 waiver of betterment contribution on new original replacement parts for vehicles aged 5 to 10 years. **Cost**: 65 RM
- **Key Replacement**: Endorsement DT5 car key and lock replacement reimbursement up to RM2,000 due to theft, robbery, or break-in. **Cost**: 50 RM
- **Flood & Theft Cleaning**: Endorsement DT6 professional cleaning reimbursement up to RM5,000 due to flood or attempted theft. **Cost**: 35 RM
- **Complete Respray**: Endorsement DT7 complete exterior spray painting coverage up to RM2,000 following accidental damage repair. **Cost**: 90 RM
- **Increased TPPD**: Endorsement 105 extension of third-party property damage limit from RM3,000,000 to RM6,000,000. **Cost**: 45 RM
- **Smart Driver DPA Bundle**: E-Assist Smart Driver comprehensive personal accident protection for driver and 4 passengers (Plan A). **Cost**: 70 RM
- **EV Smart Pack**: Section C dedicated electric vehicle bundle including charger liability, wallbox damage, and depletion towing. **Cost**: 120 RM
