# Standardized Motor Insurance Benefits Catalog (Version 4)

This catalog details the complete list of motor insurance benefits across all 7 Malaysian underwriters, categorized strictly into **Defaults (Included at RM 0 / Base Cover / Non-Tariff)** and **Add-ons (Optional Riders)** with standardized company-specific descriptions, avoiding redundant vehicle-type table splits.

> ### Policy Tier Packaging Rule (TPFT / TPO vs Comprehensive)
> - **TPFT (Third Party, Fire & Theft) & TPO (Third Party Only)**: For these specific policy tiers, **ONLY** these 3 statutory baseline benefits exist in Defaults (Included at RM 0):
>   1. **Own Damage** (Restricted to Fire & Theft for TPFT; excluded for TPO)
>   2. **Third Party Bodily Injury & Death**
>   3. **Third Party Property Damage (TPPD)**
> - **Comprehensive Policies**: In Comprehensive policies, these 3 statutory baseline benefits (`Own Damage`, `Third Party Bodily Injury & Death`, `Third Party Property Damage`) are inherent to Comprehensive cover by definition (as Comprehensive is the highest base tier). Therefore, in active Comprehensive profile catalogs, they are **omitted from the Defaults list** to prevent clutter, allowing Defaults to focus strictly on the insurer's true value-added and non-tariff inclusions (e.g. Free Towing, Betterment Waiver, Legal Defense, Key Care, All Drivers Excess Waiver, etc.).
