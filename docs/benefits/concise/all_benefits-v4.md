# Standardized Motor Insurance Benefits Catalog (Version 4)

This catalog details the complete list of motor insurance benefits across all 7 Malaysian underwriters, categorized strictly into **Defaults (Included at RM 0 / Base Cover)** and **Add-ons (Optional Riders)** with company-specific short descriptions.

---

### Lonpac Insurance (48 Total Benefits)

#### Defaults (Included at RM 0 / Base Cover):
1. **Comprehensive Accidental Own Damage, Fire & Theft** — Base indemnity covering accidental collision, overturning, fire, explosion, lightning, theft, and transit damage.
2. **Third Party Bodily Injury & Death** — Statutory unlimited legal liability coverage for bodily injury or death caused to third parties.
3. **Third Party Property Damage (TPPD)** — Statutory legal liability coverage for third-party property damage up to RM3,000,000 limit.
4. **Legal Defense Costs** — Reimburses legal fees and court representation costs incurred with insurer consent (up to RM2,000).
5. **Emergency Towing Assistance** — Built-in 24/7 emergency accident towing assistance to the nearest panel workshop (up to 200 km / RM300 base limit).
6. **24/7 Roadside Assistance & Breakdown Helpline** — Round-the-clock emergency roadside assistance for jumpstarts, battery replacements, and minor breakdown repairs.

#### Add-ons (Optional Riders):
1. **Windscreen & Window Glass Coverage (End. 89/89A)** — Repair and replacement of windscreen, window glass, and sunroof without penalty to accumulated NCD.
2. **Special Perils / Flood Coverage (End. 57)** — Comprehensive cover for loss or damage caused by flood, typhoon, landslide, storm, and natural disasters.
3. **Strike, Riot & Civil Commotion / SRCC (End. 25)** — Indemnity against physical loss or damage caused by strikers, locked-out workers, riots, or civil unrest.
4. **Compensation for Assessed Repair Time (CART) (End. 112)** — Daily cash payout for assessed workshop repair duration (e.g. 7, 14, or 21 days at RM50–RM200/day).
5. **Betterment Waiver / New Spare Parts Buyback (End. A010)** — Waives standard betterment cost-sharing percentage for vehicles 5–15 years old repaired with new genuine parts.
6. **Legal Liability of Passengers (LLOP) (End. 72)** — Protects vehicle owner against third-party liability claims resulting from negligent passenger acts.
7. **Legal Liability to Passengers / Pillion (LLP) (End. 100/108)** — Protects driver against legal liability suits for death or injury caused to passengers/pillion.
8. **Driver & Passenger Personal Accident (DPA) Bundle** — Multi-plan personal accident protection for driver and passengers (accidental death, permanent disability, medical fees).
9. **Key Replacement Cover** — Reimburses costs to replace lost/stolen smart keys, locks, and remote keyless transmitters (up to RM1,000/RM1,500).
10. **All Drivers / Unnamed Driver Excess Waiver** — Waives the compulsory RM400 excess fee when an unnamed authorized driver is involved in an accident.
11. **Increased Third Party Property Damage (TPPD) (End. 105)** — Increases third-party property damage coverage limit beyond standard RM3,000,000 (up to RM5,000,000).
12. **Personal Accident: Accidental Death** — Capital sum benefit paid to legal beneficiaries upon fatal traffic accident.
13. **Personal Accident: Permanent Disablement** — Lump-sum or scaled payout for permanent total or partial loss of limbs, sight, or bodily functions.
14. **Personal Accident: Daily Hospital Income** — Daily cash benefit for each day of inpatient hospitalization due to road accident injury (up to 30 days).
15. **Personal Accident: Medical Expenses Reimbursement** — Reimburses clinical, emergency room, and medical treatment expenses resulting from an accident.
16. **Personal Accident: Bereavement & Funeral Allowance** — Lump-sum cash payout to assist family with immediate funeral expenses upon fatal accident.
17. **Personal Accident: Double Indemnity Payout** — Doubles death and disability sum insured if accident occurs during public holidays or festivals.
18. **Personal Accident: Ambulance Fee Reimbursement** — Reimburses emergency ambulance transport charges from accident location to medical facility.
19. **Snatch Theft & Smash-and-Grab Cover** — Compensation for loss of personal handbag, wallet, or phone during snatch theft or vehicle smash-and-grab.
20. **Inconvenience & Out-of-Pocket Expense Allowance** — Daily or lump-sum financial assistance allowance to cover alternative travel costs during repair downtime.
21. **Flood Water Damage Interior Detailing & Cleaning** — Reimbursement for professional interior cabin water extraction, cleaning, and detailing after flooding (up to RM1,500).
22. **Vehicle Respray / Spray Painting Cover** — Reimburses cost of full vehicle body respray painting following an approved own-damage accident claim.
23. **EV Battery Depletion Emergency Towing** — Flatbed towing recovery to the nearest operational public EV charging station if battery depletes completely.
24. **EV Home Charger Wallbox Damage Cover** — Reimburses cost to repair or replace residential EV wallbox charger damaged by fire, theft, or accidental impact.
25. **EV Charging Bodily Injury Reimbursement** — Medical expense reimbursement for electrical shock or injuries sustained while plugging/unplugging EV charger.
26. **EV Charging Home Content & Fire Damage** — Covers residential building structural and content fire damage caused by EV charger thermal runaway.
27. **EV Home Charger Third-Party Liability** — Third-party property damage and injury liability coverage arising from residential EV charging station operations.
28. **Lonpac EV Smart Pack Bundle** — Comprehensive packaged bundle combining wallbox protection, charger liability, downtime allowance, and battery towing.
29. **Enhanced Special Perils (End. DT9)** — Enhanced natural disaster cover including fallen trees, electromechanical water ingress, and mudslide.
30. **Windscreen Auto-Reinstatement** — Automatically restores full windscreen coverage sum insured after a claim without additional reinstatement premium.
31. **Natural Falling Objects Damage Cover** — Covers physical impact damage caused by falling tree branches, fruits, or debris without requiring full special perils.
32. **Vehicle Registration & Document Replacement** — Lump-sum reimbursement to replace vehicle registration card, driving license, and NRIC lost in vehicle break-in.
33. **Legal Liability to Authorized Attendants / Loaders (End. 19i)** — Extends employer liability to cover accidental injury or death to authorized lorry attendants or loaders.
34. **Accidental Boom Damage (Mobile Cranes) (End. 38A)** — Covers accidental structural damage to mobile crane booms, outriggers, and hydraulic apparatus during work.
35. **Tool of Trade / Working Risks Extension (End. 41/42)** — Extends third-party liability coverage while mobile plant or excavators operate as working tools of trade.
36. **Accidental Overturning (Cranes & Special Equipment) (End. 38)** — Covers physical damage sustained when mobile cranes, excavators, or tippers accidentally overturn.
37. **Gas Conversion Kit & Tank Cover (NGV) (End. 97a)** — Dedicated insurance coverage for Natural Gas Vehicle (NGV) cylinders, piping, and conversion kits.
38. **Non-Standard Accessories & Equipment Cover (End. 97)** — Protects factory and custom accessories, audio units, side steps, and specialized fitments.
39. **Betterment Scale (Tariff Deductible)** — Standard tariff partial cost-sharing scale (0% under 5 years up to 40% for 10+ years) for replacing parts with new items.
40. **NCD Relief / NCD Protector (End. 111)** — Reimburses the financial value of forfeited No Claim Discount (NCD) percentage following an own-damage claim.
41. **Cross-Border Extension (Thailand / Kalimantan) (End. 101/102)** — Geographic endorsement extending comprehensive motor coverage into Thailand or Kalimantan.
42. **Ferry Transit Extension (Sabah / Labuan / Penang) (End. 109)** — Marine transit endorsement covering physical loss or damage while vehicle is on ferry/barge.
