# Master Comprehensive Motor Benefits Catalog (All 7 Insurers)

This master catalog defines the complete, standardized motor insurance benefits across all 7 Malaysian underwriters (AmAssurance, Berjaya Sompo, Etiqa, Lonpac, QBE, STMB / Takaful Malaysia, and Tune Protect).

It lists **all 63 canonical benefits** that actually exist across the underwriter catalogs, categorized cleanly with short descriptions strictly **less than 80 characters**, neutral descriptions for calculated benefits (Windscreen) and bundled packages, plus a comprehensive **Bundled Packages & Dynamic Upgrade Matrix** detailing Plan 1 to Plan 4 additions and existing benefit upgrades.

---

> ### Policy Tier Packaging Rule (TPFT / TPO vs Comprehensive)
> - **TPFT (Third Party, Fire & Theft) & TPO (Third Party Only)**: For these specific policy tiers, **ONLY** these 3 statutory baseline benefits exist in Defaults (Included at RM 0):
>   1. **Own Damage** (Restricted to Fire & Theft for TPFT; excluded for TPO)
>   2. **Third Party Bodily Injury & Death**
>   3. **Third Party Property Damage (TPPD)**
> - **Comprehensive Policies**: In Comprehensive policies, these 3 statutory baseline benefits (`Own Damage`, `Third Party Bodily Injury & Death`, `Third Party Property Damage`) are inherent to Comprehensive cover by definition (as Comprehensive is the highest base tier). Therefore, in active Comprehensive profile catalogs, they are **omitted from the Defaults list** to prevent clutter, allowing Defaults to focus strictly on the insurer's true value-added and non-tariff inclusions (e.g. Free Towing, Betterment Waiver, Legal Defense, Key Care, All Drivers Excess Waiver, etc.).

## 2. Bundled Packages & Dynamic Upgrade Matrix

This section details how **Packaged Bundles** work in the underwriting engine. When an agent or user selects a bundled package, it executes two distinct functions:
1. **Adds a bundle of new benefits / riders** (e.g. Accidental Death, Permanent Disablement, Medical Expenses, Hospital Cash, Bereavement Allowance).
2. **Dynamically upgrades existing default or add-on benefits** (e.g. Towing distance upgrades from baseline 50 km / RM 200 to 200 km, 300 km, or Unlimited towing; compulsory excess is waived).
