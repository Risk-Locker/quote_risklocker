## 2. Universal Normalization Methodology

Different insurance companies use varying brand terminology, tariff endorsement numbers, or marketing phrasing for identical underlying protections. This catalog normalizes all variations under **Single Universal Names** following Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) and Persatuan Insurans Am Malaysia (PIAM) motor tariff standards:

1. **Towing Normalization**: Terms such as `Basic Towing`, `Standard Towing`, `Emergency Towing Assistance`, and `Towing Reimbursement` are unified as **`Emergency Towing Assistance`**.
2. **Passenger Liability Normalization**: `Endorsement 100`, `Endorsement 108`, `Legal Liability to Pillion`, and `Passenger Risk` are unified as **`Legal Liability to Passengers / Pillion (LLP)`**.
3. **Third-Party Negligence Normalization**: `Endorsement 72` and `Legal Liability of Passengers` are unified as **`Legal Liability of Passengers (LLOP)`**.
4. **Betterment Normalization**: `Waiver of Betterment (End. A010)`, `Betterment Buyback (End. DT4)`, and `New Spare Part Replacement Cover` are unified as **`Betterment Waiver / New Spare Parts Buyback`**.
5. **Repair Time Loss**: `Endorsement 112`, `Compensation for Assessed Repair Time`, and `Commercial CART` are unified as **`Compensation for Assessed Repair Time (CART)`**.
6. **Proprietary Retentions**: Proprietary or non-tariff offerings with distinct legal or operational mechanisms (e.g. Etiqa's *Cashback by Driving Less*, STMB's *No Claim Cashback Hibah*, Tune Protect's *PAYD Telematics*, and branded multi-riders like *MotorShield* and *SOMPO N-hancer*) retain their specific designations as **Unique / Uncommon Benefits**.
