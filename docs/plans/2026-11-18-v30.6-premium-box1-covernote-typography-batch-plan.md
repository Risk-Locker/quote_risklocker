## Confirmed Domain & Pricing Contracts
1. **Insurance Premium = PDF Bottom Payable (Excl. Road Tax & Runner Fee):** Extraction captures the insurer's final payable (`TOTAL CONTRIBUTION`, `Total Payable`, `Total Contribution Payable`, `Total Payable Perlu Dibayar`, `Total Amount Payable`) — `Gross Premium + Service Tax + Stamp Duty`.
2. **Idempotent `applied_to_premium` Ledger (Zero Double-Counting):**
   - Each `DraftBenefitSelection` records `evidence_snapshot.applied_to_premium`.
   - Extracted PDF benefits initialize `applied_to_premium = price` because `fields.premium` already includes them.
   - Adding, editing price, or removing/unchecking a benefit in Quotation Workspace or Comparison adjusts `fields.premium` by exact RM delta (`target_effective_price - applied_to_premium`, zero extra +8% SST) and updates `applied_to_premium`. Reset restores `fields.premium.detected_value`.
   - Workspace `Total Payable` is strictly `fields.premium + effective_road_tax + effective_runner_fee` (`Total Payable` without Road Tax & Runner Fee === carbon copy of `Insurance Premium`).
3. **`round_total` Default:** `False` (exact cents by default unless Round Figure is toggled).
4. **Design 2 Box 1 <-> Top-Right 1:1 Parity:** Top-Right `Free Added Coverage` mirrors active Box 1 (`focCards`) 1:1 — zero phantom towing injection, zero skipped catalog-matched Box 1 benefits, zero hardcoded fallback perks, and zero hardcoded `RM 200`.
5. **Cover Note Full Sync & Editing + 100-PDF Hardening:** Inline + modal editing for Cover Notes in Marketing Comparison syncing to `cn_session` + `Tenure` (`won_premium`, dates, policy #, perils), plus releasing worker DB connections across Gemini HTTP waits and batching queue polls at `50` IDs.

## 10-Point Edge-Case & Scenario Matrix

| # | Dimension | Guard & Test Mapping |
| :- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Null, Empty & Unicode** | Empty Box 1 (`[]`), `None`/`""` limits, Unicode labels (`发动机排量`, `路税`), whitespace normalization (`tests/unit/test_premium_sync_box1_covernote.py`). |
| 2 | **`Decimal` Precision** | Pure `Decimal` (`ROUND_HALF_UP`) math; `applied_to_premium` idempotency; zero `float` drift; Pydantic `ge=0`; rate divide-by-zero guard when `sum_insured <= 0`. |
| 3 | **Timezones & Leap Dates** | Cover Note start/end updates preserve UTC ISO & `expiry_month` cleanly across leap years (`relativedelta`). |
| 4 | **Concurrency & Idempotency** | `expected_version` `409` conflict checks on Comparison/Covernote patches; `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` + connection release across Gemini calls; bounded hydration. |
| 5 | **Auth, RBAC & CSRF** | Read-only `GET /comparison/{id}`; `require_role` + CSRF enforced on `PATCH /{tenure_id}/entries/{id}` & `PATCH /{tenure_id}/covernote`. |
| 6 | **Malformed PDFs / Prompt Injection** | Corrupted PDFs fail-closed per job; PDS boilerplate in `authorized_driver.evidence` blocked from injecting false Special Perils. |
| 7 | **Timeouts & Partial Rollback** | Transactional rollback on partial Comparison/Covernote patch failure; Gemini 429 cooldown without pinned DB connections; 30m active bulk timeout. |
| 8 | **UI States & A4 Overflow** | Loading/saving/error/keyboard states (`Enter`/`Esc`); Design 2 A4 `scrollHeight <= clientHeight` at `1024px–1920px` & `80%–120%` zoom; zero native dialogs. |
| 9 | **Legacy DB Rows** | Drafts missing `round_total` default `False`; legacy selections initialize `applied_to_premium` safely; `081` cleans residual AmAssurance Commercial `RM 200`. |
| 10 | **Scale & Memory Limits** | `50`-ID `/jobs/status` batches, `4`-slot session hydration, `CHUNK_SIZE=3` uploads, batched `state=="current"` queries. |
